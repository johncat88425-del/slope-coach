# Screening rule (T266, fixed before any in-scope run)

A clip is **in scope** only if every condition holds, judged from its frames alone (never from a verdict):

- (a) the camera is fixed or nearly fixed for the whole clip;
- (b) the view is roughly side-on: the object does not move clearly towards or away from the camera;
- (c) one main moving object;
- (d) the object is in free flight (thrown, dropped or bouncing) for at least 0.5 s and stays fully inside the frame for that part.

Unsure means **no**, with the reason written down. Frames were read as 12 evenly spaced stills per clip.

For in-scope clips the time window is picked by a person from the frames: from just after release (or the last contact) to just before the next contact. Windows set by john for his own clips and for the Meta AI demo records are kept as they are.
