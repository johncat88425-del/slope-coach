Live demo of [slope-coach](https://github.com/johncat88425-del/slope-coach): upload a short side-on clip of a
thrown, dropped or bouncing ball, a pendulum, or a car driving past. Muse Spark asks what it needs, SAM 3.1
tracks the object, and deterministic physics code says whether the motion is consistent with the assumed
model, inconsistent, or not judgeable. It never says whether a video is real or generated.
